# My Healthy Mentor

Personal diet and training coaching context covering member onboarding, structured nutrition and workout plans, daily adherence, weekly check-ins, and AI-guided plan evolution.

## Language

### Members & Profile

**Member**:
A person authorized to use My Healthy Mentor with their own health profile, plans, check-ins, and coaching history.
_Avoid_: User, client, patient, account

**Health Profile**:
A member's structured baseline of body metrics, fitness goal, daily routine, food preferences, training background, and health limitations.
_Avoid_: Onboarding form, questionnaire, bio

**Allowlist Entry**:
An approved email address permitted to sign in as a member.
_Avoid_: Whitelist, invite code, seat

### Plans & Evolution

**Diet Plan**:
A versioned daily nutrition prescription defining calorie, macronutrient, and water targets alongside ordered meals, food portions, and substitution options.
_Avoid_: Meal plan, menu, nutrition sheet

**Training Plan**:
A versioned weekly split assigning workout sessions or rest days to each day of the week, with ordered exercises, sets, rep ranges, and rest periods.
_Avoid_: Workout routine, gym sheet, program

**Plan Draft**:
An unactivated Diet Plan or Training Plan generated by the Mentor or parsed from imported text, awaiting member review and editing before activation.
_Avoid_: Template, preview, staging plan

**Plan Proposal**:
A structured change to an active Diet Plan or Training Plan recommended by the Mentor, presenting before-and-after differences and requiring explicit member approval before taking effect.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, auto-update, edit request

**Plan Version**:
An archived, read-only snapshot of a previously active Diet Plan or Training Plan preserved when a newer version is activated.
_Avoid_: Backup, revision, history item

### Daily & Weekly Tracking

**Daily Log**:
A member's record for a single calendar date capturing diet adherence, training adherence, any same-day workout override, and contextual notes.
_Avoid_: Diary, journal, daily entry

**Workout Override**:
A single-day substitution of the scheduled training session for another session or rest day that leaves the base Training Plan unchanged.
_Avoid_: Split edit, reschedule

**Daily Note**:
A concise contextual record attached to a Daily Log capturing same-day adaptations or deviations, such as a planned off-plan meal.
_Avoid_: Comment, memo, deviation log

**Weekly Check-in**:
A periodic progress submission containing fasting weight, optional measurements and subjective notes, and progress photos.
_Avoid_: Weigh-in, weekly update, progress report

**Progress Photo**:
A private body-evolution photograph attached to a Weekly Check-in for side-by-side visual comparison across dates.
_Avoid_: Evolution picture, body scan, check-in image

### Coaching & Memory

**Mentor**:
The AI diet and training coach that advises a member, answers ad-hoc questions, reviews weekly check-ins, and drafts plans or proposals.
_Avoid_: Bot, assistant, agent

**Mentor Thread**:
A member's single continuous coaching conversation shared across all surfaces.
_Avoid_: Chat log, session, room

**Conversation Summary**:
A condensed rolling synopsis of older Mentor Thread messages kept so long-term coaching context persists without replaying full history.
_Avoid_: Memory block, chat archive, RAG chunk

**Daily Digest**:
A scheduled summary presenting a member's training session and diet plan for the current day.
_Avoid_: Morning notification, schedule ping, daily reminder
